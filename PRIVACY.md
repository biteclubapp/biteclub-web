# BiteClub - Eat Together Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to BiteClub - Eat Together (the "App"), the consumer mobile app operated under the BiteClub brand. You can use some features, such as Decide, without creating an account. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

**✓ No Tracking.** We don't track you across apps or websites.

**🔒 Your Data.** Contact info, content and social connections stay yours.

**🚫 No Ads.** No third-party advertising or data selling.

### Device Permissions
- **Camera:** to take photos of your meals
- **Photos/Library:** to upload existing photos
- **Notifications:** to send cooking reminders and updates you care about, like a friend cooking your recipe
- **Contacts (optional):** to find friends
- **Microphone (optional):** only if you import a recipe from a voice note

You can change permissions in your device settings at any time.

## How We Use Information
- Provide and secure the App, authenticate users and maintain your account
- Enable social features (following, posting, commenting, liking)
- Personalize Decide: we use your swipes, saves, picks and cooks to rank which meals Decide shows you, only within BiteClub
- Turn links, text, photos and voice notes you import into recipes, using AI service providers
- Deliver push notifications and reminders you configure
- Understand and improve the App with product analytics
- Improve performance, debug issues and protect against abuse, including automated checks of uploaded photos

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. Our AI providers process imported content only to return results to us.

## Sharing and Disclosures
We share data with service providers acting on our behalf:
- **Supabase:** authentication and database
- **Twilio (via Supabase Auth):** sending the SMS one-time passcode when you sign in with a phone number
- **Apple and Google:** authentication if you choose Sign in with Apple or Google
- **Cloudflare Images/Stream:** media storage and delivery
- **Cloudflare Workers:** media API, scheduled cleanup, and recipe-import processing
- **Expo:** push notification delivery
- **PostHog (United States):** product analytics and error tracking
- **OpenRouter, with Google (Gemini) and OpenAI models:** turning recipes you import into structured recipes, and recipe matching
- **OpenAI:** automated safety checks of uploaded photos

We may disclose information to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer.

## Your Choices
- **Analytics:** after you sign in, turn product analytics off in Settings. If you use the App as a guest, analytics may still be collected until you sign in and turn them off.
- **Notifications:** control categories and reminder times, or opt out any time.
- **Contacts:** skip contacts access; no matching without your consent.
- **Account:** access, update or delete your account in Settings.
- **Data requests:** contact us for data access or deletion requests.

## Retention and Deletion
- Your content and profile persist until you delete them or your account.
- Analytics events are kept for 1 year. Deleting your account does not immediately delete them; they expire after 1 year, or you can contact us to request deletion.
- When you delete your account, we clear Decide preferences stored on your device. Logging out also clears Decide preferences on the device.
- Push tokens are kept only while notifications are enabled.
- Contacts matching works at query time; numbers aren't stored beyond the lookup.
- Backups and logs keep limited data for a short period for security and reliability.
- When you delete your account, media deletions may take up to about 1 hour to propagate.

## Children's Privacy
The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided us personal data, contact us to delete it.

## International Transfers
